Catering & event questions

How much does Indian catering cost per person?

A small tray serves 15–20. A basic lunch (small chicken tray $100, small vegetable tray $75, small jeera rice $40, 20 naan at $2.99) is about $275, roughly $14–18 a head before delivery. Add a second meat and a dessert and it lands near $22–25. Extra-large trays serve 45–50; a chicken XL is $200. For more than 50 guests, order several XL trays or a full-service buffet — there’s no upper limit. Full-service buffets are quoted by headcount and staffing; fill out the catering form and we’ll get back to you.
